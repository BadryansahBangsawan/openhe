# OpenHE logo

The symbol is the "O" of OpenHE drawn as a horseshoe magnet: a ring that is open at the top, with both
pole tips cut off. It stands for the magnet in a Hall-effect switch and for "open" software.

## Files (`final/`)

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `symbol-color.svg` | Amber symbol, transparent background. For dark surfaces. |
| `symbol-on-dark.svg` | Amber symbol on the dark `#0F1013` square. Avatars, social images. |
| `symbol-black.svg`, `symbol-white.svg` | One-colour versions. |
| `symbol-small-{color,black,white}.svg` | Small-size cut (see below). |
| `lockup-horizontal-color-on-dark.svg` | Symbol as the "O" + "penHE", amber and `#ECEDEF`, transparent. |
| `lockup-horizontal-color-on-light.svg` | Same, with `#0F1013` letters for light surfaces. |
| `lockup-horizontal-on-dark.svg` | Dark `#0F1013` background included. |
| `lockup-horizontal-black.svg`, `lockup-horizontal-white.svg` | One-colour versions. |

The wordmark is outlined paths (Inter Bold for "pen", Inter Regular for "HE", SIL Open Font License 1.1).
There is no live text in any file.

App icons live in `../icons/`: the amber symbol on a `#0F1013` rounded-square tile
(`openhe.icns`, `openhe.ico`, `png/openhe-{16..1024}.png`).

## Colours

| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Accent (symbol) | `#FFB224` |
| Dark background | `#0F1013` |
| Text on dark | `#ECEDEF` |
| Tile border | `#2C3038` |

Amber on white has a contrast ratio of about 1.8:1. On light backgrounds use the black version of the
symbol whenever the symbol must be clearly legible, and keep the amber version for large, decorative use.

## Clear space and minimum size

- Clear space: keep at least one ring thickness (about 23 % of the symbol's diameter) free on every side of the symbol or lockup.
- Minimum size: symbol 24 px wide (use the small-size cut below 48 px); lockup 120 px wide.
- Below 24 px use the app icon or the small-size cut only, never the master drawing.

## Small-size cut

At 16 to 48 px the master's thin slits between the pole tips close up. The small-size cut has a thicker
ring, a wider top opening (46 degrees instead of 30) and 17-unit slits, so the magnet still reads at 16 px.
The icon PNGs of 32 px and smaller use it.

## Do not

- Do not recolour the symbol outside the colours above.
- Do not close the opening, fill the slits, rotate or mirror the symbol.
- Do not set the wordmark in another typeface or re-typeset it with live text.
- Do not add shadows, gradients or outlines.
- Do not place the amber symbol on a busy photo or a light-yellow background.

## Name

Write the name as "OpenHE": "Open" is bold, "HE" is regular weight. In running text use plain "OpenHE".
OpenHE is not affiliated with AULA. Do not use AULA marks with it.
